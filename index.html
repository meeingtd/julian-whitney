Where I’ve been

Each place gave me a new understanding of human consciousness, and a greater sense that even though we all come from different places, we share one moment, the present.

    I grew up kind of all over the place. I’m from Concord, Massachusetts, and spent four years abroad in Doha, Qatar, where I went to Lycée Bonaparte, learned French and got to see the world. I’m Algerian, ethnically French and American, and that mix is a big part of me. I moved to North Carolina for four years of school at Wake Forest, with a semester in Barcelona, and spending time kind of everywhere has been a highlight of my life. Now I work in FP&A at an aerospace and defense manufacturer and build tools with Claude Code.
